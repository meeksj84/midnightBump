# Midnight Bump Style Guide

## Backgrounds
- Dark charcoal
- Semi-transparent when possible

## Text
- Warm cream
- Avoid pure white

## Accent
- Teal for informational items
- Amber for active elements
- Crimson only for warnings

## Borders
- Thin (1–2 px)
- Soft gray

## Corners
- 10–12 px rounded

## Blur
- Moderate, never excessive

## Animation
- Smooth and restrained

## Overall Feel
- Quiet
- Nostalgic
- Cinematic


## Typography

### UI
IBM Plex Sans

### Terminal
Iosevka Nerd Font

### Japanese
Noto Sans CJK

### Sizes

Waybar: 11
Kitty: 12
Wofi: 12
Dunst: 11

### Rules

- Avoid bold unless emphasizing something important.
- Prefer regular and medium weights.
- Use sentence case instead of ALL CAPS.
- Keep spacing generous.
